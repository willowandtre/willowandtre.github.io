Our Story

Precision Craftsmanship & Custom Expression

Welcome to Willow & Tre

Established with a commitment to quality and detail, Willow & Tre specializes in high-quality commercial embroidery, custom heat-press apparel, and brand digitizing.

We believe that apparel and gear should be an expression of your passion. Whether you need branded uniforms for your business, custom embroidered hats, or tailored pieces for an event, we bring precision craftsmanship to every stitch and print.

Operating with commercial-grade multi-needle embroidery machines and precision heat press equipment, we handle projects of all sizes with dedicated care and crisp, vibrant results.

We are a women- and minority-owned company and we are proud to be your go-to apparel printing shop
